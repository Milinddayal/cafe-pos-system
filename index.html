Our Story

Started with one idea: vegetarian food shouldn't be an afterthought.

Green Fusion began as a family table — recipes passed down, refined, and finally opened up to the neighbourhood. Every plate is built vegetarian-first, not vegetarian-only, and every drink is made to order, from the espresso bar to the mocktail shaker. Milind runs it hands-on, because he believes a cafe shouldn't make you choose between great food and great coffee.

"We wanted a place where a quick coffee and a full meal feel equally cared for."
